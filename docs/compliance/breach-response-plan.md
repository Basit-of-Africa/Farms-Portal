# Personal Data Breach Response Plan

A **personal data breach** is any security incident that leads to personal data being accidentally or unlawfully destroyed, lost, altered, disclosed or accessed. Examples: a lost laptop, a mis-sent email with investor details, a leaked database export, a compromised admin account, or a public link to a contract.

## Timelines

| Who | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) | **Within 72 hours** of becoming aware, if the breach is likely to risk individuals' rights and freedoms | NDPA 2023 / GAID 2025 |
| Affected individuals | **Immediately / without undue delay** if the breach is likely to be **high risk** (e.g. fraud, identity theft, exposure of sensitive data) | NDPA 2023 / GAID 2025 |
| EU/UK supervisory authority | Within 72 hours, if EU/UK residents are affected and GDPR applies | GDPR Art. 33-34 |

The 72-hour clock starts when you **become aware** of the breach, not when the investigation ends. You can notify in phases.

## Steps

1. **Contain (first hour).** Revoke access: deactivate or reset the account (a password reset revokes all sessions), rotate keys and secrets, remove public links, recall emails.
2. **Record.** Log the incident under **Privacy & Data → Breaches** with the time you became aware. The register shows the NDPC deadline.
3. **Assess.** What data was involved, how many people, could it cause fraud, identity theft, financial loss or distress? Use the audit log (**Privacy & Data → Audit log**) to see who accessed what.
4. **Notify the NDPC** if the risk test is met. Include: what happened, the categories and approximate number of people and records, likely consequences, measures taken, and the DPO's contact details. Click "Mark NDPC notified".
5. **Notify individuals** if high risk. Use plain language and say what they should do (e.g. watch for phishing, change passwords). Click "Mark individuals notified".
6. **Fix and learn.** Record the containment actions, update the DPIA and controls, and close the incident.

Record **every** breach in the register, including ones you decide not to notify, together with the reasoning.
