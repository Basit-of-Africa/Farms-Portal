# Handling Data Subject Requests

People can make requests through **Privacy & Data → Make a privacy request** (signed-in users), or by email or letter to the DPO. Log requests received outside the portal too. The simplest way is to ask the person to submit them in the portal.

**Deadline:** the portal sets a due date (default 30 days; change it under Privacy & Data → Settings). Confirm the deadline that applies with your DPO. Respond without undue delay.

## Before acting

- **Verify identity.** Portal requests come from a signed-in account. For email requests, verify identity before releasing or erasing anything.
- **Check exemptions.** For example, records the law requires you to keep, or data that would reveal other people.

## By request type

| Request | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Access / portability** | The person can download an instant JSON copy with "Download my data". For a manual request, generate the export, add anything held outside the portal, and send it securely. |
| **Rectification** | Users can correct their name, email and phone themselves. Admins correct other fields (plots, amounts) through the normal screens. |
| **Erasure** | Requests → **Erase data**, then type the username to confirm. This anonymises the profile, deletes messages addressed to the person, de-identifies comments and notes, removes IP addresses from the audit trail and revokes sessions. Contracts, investment and financial records are **kept without identifiers** for the legal retention period; tell the person this. Data in backups expires through backup rotation. |
| **Restriction** | Deactivate the account and note the restriction. Don't process the data except to store it until the issue is resolved. |
| **Objection** | Stop processing based on legitimate interests unless there are compelling legitimate grounds; record your reasoning. For direct marketing, stop at once. |
| **Withdraw consent** | The person can untick promotional messages themselves; otherwise do it for them. |

Mark the request **Completed** or **Rejected**; a reason is required for rejections. The person is emailed automatically, including how to complain to the NDPC or their local authority.
